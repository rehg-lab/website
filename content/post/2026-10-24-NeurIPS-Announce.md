Four papers accepted for publication at the *40th Conference on Neural Information Processing Systems* ([NeurIPS 2026](https://neurips.cc/)):
* J. Kim, X. Cao, H. Yang, B. Boote, A. Jojic, F. Ryan, B. Lai, S. Lee, J. M. Rehg. GRASP: Learning to Ground Social Reasoning in Multi-Person Non-Verbal Interactions.
* B. N. Zhao, O. Kara, J. Kim, J. M. Rehg. ST-DiffEye: Diffusion-based Continuous Gaze Generation via Joint Scanpath-Trajectory Modeling.
* B. Li, Y. Shen, Y. Liu, Y. Xu, J. Liu, X. Li, Z. Li, J. Zhu, Y. Zhong, F. Lan, J. Cao, J. M. Rehg, H. Ji, I. Lourentzou, X. Cao. Toward Cognitive Supersensing in Multimodal Large Language Models.
* A. Mammadov, O. Kara, K. Oktay, I. Azangulov, A. K. Akan, H. Chung, J. M. Rehg, Y. W. Teh. Exact Posterior Score Estimation for Solving Linear Inverse Problems.

* Y. Liu, N. Huang, X. Cao, J. M. Rehg. Thinking in Pictures: A Systematic Benchmark for Reasoning-driven Image Generation.
